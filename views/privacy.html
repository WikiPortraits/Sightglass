Privacy Policy

Wikimedia Sightglass is hosted on Wikimedia Toolforge, and its use is covered by the Wikimedia Cloud Services Terms of Use.

When you log in with your Wikimedia account, the tool stores your username and user ID, and queries you run are associated with your account. Tool maintainers can see which users ran which queries for the purposes of maintaining and debugging the tool.

Query results are not private: anyone with a link to a results page can view it.
